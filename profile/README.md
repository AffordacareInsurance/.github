# AffordaCare Insurance

AffordaCare Insurance is a Dallas, TX-based insurance brokerage focused on helping individuals, families, and employers access affordable major medical, life, dental, and vision coverage.

## What we do

- Individual and family health insurance guidance
- Employer health plan strategy and cost optimization
- Supplemental coverage including life, dental, and vision options
